## Preparation
- **Fasting 10–12 h** (water is fine): fasting glucose, lipid profile. Book an early-morning slot.
- **No fasting**: HbA1c, thyroid (TSH, T3/T4), CBC, vitamin D, B12. Thyroid is best in the morning, before thyroid medicine.
- Keep taking regular medicines unless the doctor said otherwise; on diabetes medicines, take them after the sample when fasting.
