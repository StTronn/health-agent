# Medicines

Sites: Zepto (zepto.com) for quick delivery, including prescription medicines when the family has a prescription (they upload it and pay through the live view link); see `sites/zepto.md`.

## Picking the product
- Match the form to the person: children get syrup or suspension (check the strength, e.g. paracetamol 120 mg/5 ml or 250 mg/5 ml), adults tablets.
- Smallest pack that covers the need; check the expiry if it's shown.
