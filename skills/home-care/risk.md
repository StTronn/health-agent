# Risk: who needs a lower threshold

- **Infants under 3 months**: any fever of 38°C / 100.4°F or more is an emergency.
- **Children under 5**: unusually drowsy or floppy, refusing all fluids, or fever beyond 5 days needs a doctor at least.
- **Adults over 65, or with diabetes, heart, kidney or lung disease, or a weak immune system**: see a doctor sooner for vomiting, diarrhoea, fever or a wound that isn't healing. Dehydration hits harder.
- **Diabetes**: sugars persistently above 300 mg/dL, or a foot wound, needs a doctor. Confusion with high sugar is an emergency. While vomiting or not eating, medicines may need adjusting, which is a doctor's decision.
- **Pregnancy**: bleeding, severe tummy pain, severe headache with vision changes, or reduced baby movements is an emergency.
