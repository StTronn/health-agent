# Everyday medicines

- **Paracetamol**: 15 mg/kg per dose, at least 4 hours apart, no more than 4 doses in 24 hours. Check the strength on the label (e.g. 120 mg/5 ml or 250 mg/5 ml) and work out the volume. Adults: 500 mg–1 g per dose, up to 4 g a day.
- **Ibuprofen**: 10 mg/kg per dose, every 6–8 hours, with food, for children over 3 months and 5 kg. Avoid when dehydrated, in kidney disease, on blood-pressure medicines, or in adults over 65 without a doctor's advice.
- **Don't**: give leftover or someone else's antibiotics (and check allergies first); give aspirin to anyone under 16; give anti-vomiting medicines to young children without a doctor.
- **Check allergies and current medicines** on the member's file before suggesting anything.
- **Stopping, starting or changing a prescribed medicine** is a doctor's decision.
