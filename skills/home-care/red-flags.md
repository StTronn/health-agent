# Red flags (emergency)

Any of these means `emergency`.

- **Breathing**: struggling to breathe, can't speak in full sentences, ribs pulling in, grunting, blue or grey lips or face, choking.
- **Heart / circulation**: chest pain or pressure (especially spreading to the arm, jaw or back, or with sweating or nausea), fainting with chest pain or palpitations, severe bleeding that won't stop, cold mottled skin.
- **Brain / nerves**: face drooping, arm weakness, slurred speech, sudden confusion, sudden worst-ever headache, a headache with stiff neck and fever, a seizure (first-ever, or longer than 5 minutes), unresponsive or very hard to wake.
- **Infection**: a non-blanching rash (doesn't fade under a pressed glass) with fever, signs of sepsis (very unwell, mottled, fast breathing, confusion).
- **Abdomen**: vomiting blood, black or tarry stools, severe abdominal pain with a rigid belly.
- **Dehydration**: no urine for 12h or more, no tears, sunken eyes, very lethargic.
- **Injury / poisoning**: major trauma or head injury with vomiting or confusion, a suspected overdose or poisoning, severe allergic reaction (lip, tongue or throat swelling, wheeze).
- **Mental health**: thoughts of suicide or self-harm with intent or a plan.
